A PLAN, A PATTERN, A QUESTION

Give the quiet part a purpose.

Players can talk while choosing their plan. Once the questions arrive, only their own question may determine their planned answer. The phone covers each private turn before passing to the next person.

What the phone is doing

These are ordinary classical simulations of ideal quantum distributions. The phone can see all the questions; a real Bell experiment has stricter physical conditions. Each modeled local result is random, so the correlations cannot send a chosen instant message.

The four-card challenge audits a locked plan. The classroom lab samples fresh, independent random questions. No learning-gain claim is made.
